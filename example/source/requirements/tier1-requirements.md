# Tier 1 Requirements

Tier 1 requirements say what DemoScan does for the people who use it. Each one traces to the
intended use, and the platform requirements derive from them through their `Parent:` lines.

## REQ-T1-001: Secure and correct study intake

Authorised clinical staff sign in to DemoScan and import MRI studies, and every imported study
stays associated with the right patient.

IUS Source: Installation & User Specification, Installation checklist.

## REQ-T1-002: Validated analysis and reporting

DemoScan analyses imported MRI studies with validated pipelines and exports the results in a
standard format.

IUS Source: Installation & User Specification, Operational monitoring.
